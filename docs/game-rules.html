2. How the planner decides

Cost of a plan

  • Raw material cost plus machines: one machine is worth 300 coins (1000 with the cauldron on).
  • A heated machine counts as 2 machines (the heater under it).
  • A cauldron slot without a plant counts +4, a slot sharing its item with another slot +4.

Belts and limits

  • A belt carries its speed in total, mixed items or not. Player
  • One belt per machine input (Linen: two). Data
  • Fluids in pipes have no cap. Player
  • Coins always on one line; you feed them. Player

Whole machines

0.47 nurseries needed → 1 built. Its extra output is spare capacity: the belt backs up and the nursery waits, nothing is lost. Player

3. Nurseries and self-made fertilizer

  • Growth time = nutrients of all outputs ÷ the fertilizer's nutrients per second. Data
  • Fertilizer per cycle = nutrients ÷ (nutrient value × Fertilizer Efficiency). Data
  • All outputs leave on one belt: a nursery makes at most one belt (165/min at level 7). Player
  • Seeds stay in the nursery. Chamomile is very hungry. Player
  • Own nurseries get full priority, only leftovers leave, plus a 25 % reserve. Player

4. Heat

Heaters

  • Heated machines must stand on a heating device. Data
  • Stone Stove: 1 heat/s itself, 9 slots. Steam Heating Pad: 9 slots. Calc
  • Separated steam pads need a pipe between them. Player
  • Heaters sit under the machines' columns, not on a grid. Calc

Fuel per craft

Machine heat × working time, plus the heater's own burn × (slots ÷ 9). That heater share doesn't speed up with research. Assumed Fuel = heat ÷ (heat value × Fuel Efficiency).

5. Cauldron, Advanced Cauldron, Paradox Crucible

Cauldron mix

  • Three inputs; their values are summed (×0.65 if two match, ×0.5 if all three match); out comes the material whose target is nearest. Calc
  • Only the result comes out, never an input. Player
  • Three different inputs: a doubled item fills its first slot completely before the second. Player
  • Plant mixes first. Best Clay: Redcurrant + Sage + Sage Powder (saves heating pads). Player
  • Filters set in the blueprint (CauldronFilterName). Blueprint

Advanced Cauldron & Paradox

  • Advanced: same two items → next target above; two different → target nearest the difference, below the higher one. Calc
  • Paradox Crucible: brew time = 1500 ÷ (stack × value), between 0.5 s and 1500 s; anything → Oblivion Essence → Vitality Essence; 1200 heat/s. Calc

6. Other machines

  • Enhanced Grinder: twice the speed of a grinder. Player
  • Grind at the nursery: the grinder sits right at the nursery's output when it takes all of it. Player
  • Brine: 4000/min from rock salt, not athanors; pipe machines may stack. Player
  • Purchasing Portal: one item each, paid in coins (1 silver = 1000 copper, 1 gold = 100 silver); speed assumed one belt. Assumed
  • Sinks: Knowledge Altar, Trash Barrel (input at height 3), Dispatch Portal. Data

7. Belts, splits and the starter box

  • Player blueprints use only the plain belt (turns, splits, merges too); BaseActiveIndex = connected sides relative to the front. Blueprint
  • Filter Splitter: tall → filtered item on the lower output; flat → on the side output. Blueprint
  • Balanced Splitter: in the files, but hidden and not unlockable. Data
  • Lifts: in at the bottom end, out at the top end; one item path per lift. Data

9. Building and height

  • A storey is 16 cells; the top cell is the ceiling's. Default module height: 15. Player
  • Machines rest on grinder-sized machines, lab stands, beams, wood blocks, stoves, never on other machines; cauldrons stack on cauldrons. Blueprint
  • Raised belts need supports: lab stands (a belt shares their cell) or wood blocks. Blueprint
  • Raw materials come in at the module's front, products leave at the back.
  • Buildings unlock by level 1–10; byproducts sharing a belt need Filter Splitters. Data

10. Your design rules for the generator

  • Compact: little free space between modules and portals; use height, but flat when that's simpler (grinder towers). Player
  • Same machine kinds together; don't mix stacks without need. Player
  • Generation must always finish (effort counted in routing steps, same plan → same module).
  • Feed evenly where several machines start from one supply, above all for starter-primed items. Player
  • Splitting at the end of a chain goes behind the construct, where there is room. Player

11. Blueprint file format

The body lists buildings with rotation, pivot, BaseActiveIndex, RecipeName, SupplyName, FilterName, CauldronFilterName, SeedName, CustomColor. Pipes: side bits like belts, +16 down, +32 up. 12. What we don't know yet

  • High3-way belt split: does straight win over both sides, and how do the sides share?
  • HighMerges (plain belt merge, Priority Merger): which input goes first, and is it fair?
  • HighMachine input buffers: how many items a nursery or cauldron holds; decides how big the starter box fill must be.
  • MediumPurchasing Portal speed: assumed one belt.
  • MediumHeater burn with research: assumed not sped up (5–9 % fuel difference otherwise).
  • MediumLifts: height and throughput limits, taking or giving items mid-shaft, why stacked cauldrons can't share one.
  • MediumSteam pads: which pads heat through neighbours and which need their own pipe.
  • MediumUntaught machines: Shaper/Advanced Shaper (front output), Aether Shaper, Arcane Processor, Omni Machine, many-output Refiners, Paradox Crucible in modules.
  • LowStone Furnace (42 slots, 4 heat/s): how it's built and fed.
  • LowPipes: throughput or length limits (treated as unlimited).
  • LowThermal Extractor height bonus and Advanced Athanor catalysts: not modelled.
  • LowCatapults, coins on belts (starfi5h: 50× a belt), seed counts: unverified.
  • LowGame data freshness: items and recipes come from an external export; re-run the extractor after a patch.
